Inertable is a headless datatable for Laravel and Inertia. Sorting, filtering, and pagination are handled on the server; the markup is left entirely to you, so a table can look like whatever the page already looks like. It ships on Packagist with a test suite and a style check running on every push. Every figure here comes from Packagist and the repository.

About

I work at Lamaau Labs in Jakarta. Most of what I ship is tooling: a datatable package on Packagist, a face-search app that runs entirely offline, a placeholder image service, and a pile of small utilities that scratch an itch. The interesting part is rarely the model. It is the ingest, the cache, and the one field that arrives as a string on Tuesdays.

Everything I build runs on infrastructure I can read end to end: Postgres, SQLite, Hono, Docker, one VPS, and a reverse proxy. I would rather ship a boring system that survives contact with real data than a clever one that needs a babysitter.
